AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. Becomes an Official BOAT Apprenticeship Partner

In Association with the Government of Tamil Nadu & the Directorate of Technical Education (DOTE)

Table of Contents

On this page

Introduction Key Disciplines Success Stories How to Apply

AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is proud to announce its official partnership with the Board of Apprenticeship Training (BOAT) under the Government of India, marking a significant milestone in our commitment to developing the next generation of skilled engineering professionals.

As an official BOAT Apprenticeship Partner, AARAA Infrastructure provides structured apprenticeship opportunities that combine classroom knowledge with practical industry experience. Eligible diploma and engineering graduates gain hands-on exposure to live Civil, Interior, and MEP projects while developing technical, professional, and leadership competencies required by the modern construction industry.

"Together, we are not just building infrastructure—we are building careers, nurturing talent, and shaping the future of engineering excellence."
— Alekhya / Business Head
