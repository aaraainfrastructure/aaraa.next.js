Aaraa Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is pleased to announce its engagement with Continuum Green Energy Limited for the execution of ICR (Inverter Control Room) civil foundation works for a utility-scale solar power project at Toravi, Karnataka.

The work order entrusts Aaraa Infrastructure with the construction of critical civil foundations and associated infrastructure required for the project. The scope covers two blocks, with works to be executed in accordance with the approved project drawings, technical specifications, quality requirements, and HSE standards.

Our Scope of Work

Aaraa Infrastructure's responsibility extends beyond basic concrete works. The assigned scope encompasses several interconnected civil and site activities necessary for establishing the project's foundation infrastructure.

Key Civil & Execution Activities:

  • Construction of ICR civil foundations as per approved engineering drawings.
  • Foundation works for IDT and inverter-related electrical infrastructure.
  • HT Panel Foundation complete with protective shed, canopy, and safety hand railing.
  • ICO / ICOA Panel Foundation complete with shed, canopy, and hand railing.
  • Auxiliary transformer foundation civil works.
  • NIFPS-related specialized civil works.
  • Main control room civil construction works.
  • Area marking and precision setting out using total-station surveying equipment.
  • Gravel filling and associated switchyard yard development.
  • Soil backfilling, layer-by-layer compaction, and site levelling.
  • Structural excavation and concrete casting activities.
  • Precision grouting of transformers as per approved engineering specifications.
  • Cable-entry related civil provisions and trench penetrations.
  • Continuous housekeeping, waste management, and site maintenance.
  • Material handling, logistics, and equipment shifting within the project site.

Engineering with Quality at the Core

Solar power projects demand a high level of coordination between civil, structural, and electrical requirements. Foundation accuracy, concrete strength, equipment positioning, and strict adherence to approved drawings are essential for reliable long-term performance.

For this assignment, the work order specifies M25 concrete, with a minimum curing period of 7 days, along with stringent requirements for quality testing and inspection. Aaraa Infrastructure's scope includes full quality-control equipment and testing arrangements on site, including cube moulds, concrete vibrators, and slump testing equipment.

Safety & HSE Compliance

Safety is an integral part of our execution approach. The project requirements call for strict compliance with the client's QHSE requirements, applicable HSE norms, and statutory safety regulations. Site personnel, heavy machinery movement, material handling, and concrete pouring activities follow prescribed safety protocols.

Aaraa Infrastructure deploys dedicated site personnel, including experienced site engineers, supervisors, and HSE officers, to maintain safe and controlled execution throughout the 15-day sprint.
