Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our commercial & industrial construction services in Chennai

What services does AARAA Infrastructure provide in Chennai?
AARAA Infrastructure provides civil construction, commercial building construction, industrial infrastructure, turnkey EPC, PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) fabrication & erection, solar & renewable energy infrastructure, MEP services, and commercial interior fit-outs in Chennai.
Does AARAA undertake industrial construction projects?
Yes, AARAA Infrastructure specializes in industrial construction projects including manufacturing plants, factory structures, warehouses, industrial sheds, and heavy civil foundations across industrial hubs like Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Ambattur, and Guindy.
Does AARAA provide commercial construction services?
Yes, we offer complete commercial construction services in Chennai, delivering corporate office buildings, retail facilities, institutional spaces, and mixed-use commercial developments from engineering planning to turnkey handover.
Does AARAA provide EPC construction services?
Yes, AARAA Infrastructure offers single-point Engineering, Procurement, and Construction (EPC) services, managing design, material procurement, civil execution, MEP integration, and project commissioning.
Does AARAA provide PEB construction services?
Yes, we are a PEB construction company in Chennai, handling design, structural steel fabrication, site erection, and turnkey construction for pre-engineered buildings, warehouses, and industrial sheds.
Does AARAA undertake solar EPC projects?
Yes, AARAA Infrastructure delivers civil and structural infrastructure for large-scale solar EPC projects, ground-mounted solar power plants, and renewable energy installations.
Does AARAA work on renewable-energy projects?
Yes, we provide civil engineering and infrastructure setup for renewable energy projects, including solar EPC and wind-solar hybrid infrastructure across Tamil Nadu.
Does AARAA provide MEP services?
Yes, our MEP division delivers mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC installations for commercial, industrial, and institutional developments in Chennai.
Does AARAA undertake turnkey construction?
Yes, we specialize in turnkey construction, delivering end-to-end management from architectural conceptualization and engineering design to structural construction, MEP fit-out, and final handover.
Which areas of Chennai does AARAA serve?
We execute projects across Chennai and key industrial clusters, including Guindy, Ambattur, Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Tambaram, OMR, ECR, Sholinganallur, Perungudi, Porur, Manali, Ennore, Poonamallee, and Maraimalai Nagar.
How can I request a construction project consultation?
You can click 'Get Project Consultation' on this page to fill out our project enquiry form, or reach out directly to AARAA Infrastructure at our Guindy office or via phone (+91 868 100 3111).
Does AARAA undertake infrastructure projects?
Yes, AARAA Infrastructure executes major civil infrastructure projects including water treatment plants, SWRO desalination facilities civil works, roads, external utilities, and heavy engineering developments.
