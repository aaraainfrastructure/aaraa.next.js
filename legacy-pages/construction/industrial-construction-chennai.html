FAQ

Industrial Construction in Chennai — Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about scope, delivery, pricing and execution.

What does an industrial construction company do?

An industrial construction company builds manufacturing and process facilities. AARAA Infrastructure covers site development, foundations, factory structures, industrial flooring, utility buildings, plant services and external infrastructure.

Does AARAA build greenfield factories in Chennai?

Yes. Greenfield industrial facilities can be delivered from land development through structure, flooring, utilities and services to handover, along with brownfield expansions within operating plants.

Which industrial corridors around Chennai does AARAA serve?

AARAA supports industrial projects in and around Chennai including Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Irungattukottai, Maraimalai Nagar, Gummidipoondi, Ambattur and Manali, subject to project scope.

Can AARAA construct heavy machine foundations?

Yes. Machine foundations, pile caps, raft foundations and vibration-sensitive equipment bases can be executed to the approved structural and vendor design requirements.

What industrial flooring options are available?

Options include VDF, trimix, power-floated concrete, epoxy coatings, PU flooring and polished concrete, selected based on loading, chemical exposure, hygiene needs and flatness tolerance.

Is PEB or RCC better for a factory building?

PEB usually suits large clear-span, faster-erection requirements, while RCC may be preferred for heavy sustained loading, fire considerations or multi-storey layouts. The choice follows the structural brief and process needs.

Can AARAA work inside an operating plant?

Yes. Brownfield work is planned around production schedules, shutdown windows, permit-to-work systems, restricted access and safety segregation from live operations.
