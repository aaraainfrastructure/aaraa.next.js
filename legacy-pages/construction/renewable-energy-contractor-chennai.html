Documentation & Handover

Pour records, test reports, as-built drawings and stage-wise handover documentation.

FAQ

Renewable Energy Contractor in Chennai — Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about scope, delivery, pricing and execution.

What does a renewable energy contractor do?

A renewable energy contractor executes the construction scope required to build generation assets. AARAA Infrastructure delivers civil and structural works including turbine foundations, solar plant civil works, access roads, substation civil scope and evacuation infrastructure.

What renewable projects has AARAA delivered?

AARAA has executed civil works for a 180 MWp solar power project in Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu and a 140.6 MW wind-solar hybrid power project, including utility-scale civil engineering and turbine foundation works.

Does AARAA construct wind turbine foundations?

Yes. Wind turbine foundation works including reinforcement, embedded ring or anchor cage installation, formwork and controlled mass concrete pours can be executed to the approved structural design.

Can AARAA handle both solar and wind scope on hybrid projects?

Yes. AARAA has worked on a wind-solar hybrid project where both turbine foundations and solar block civil works were required, along with shared access and evacuation infrastructure.

Does the scope include substation and evacuation civil work?

It can. Equipment plinths, control room buildings, cable trenches, switchyard civil works and transmission structure foundations can be included depending on the agreed contract scope.

How are remote renewable sites mobilised?

Remote sites require planning for site establishment, batching arrangements, water sourcing, labour accommodation, plant and machinery movement and material logistics. This is assessed during pre-mobilisation for each project.

What information is needed to quote a renewable project?

Technology type, capacity, site location, land area, geotechnical report if available, layout drawings, structural design basis and target schedule.
