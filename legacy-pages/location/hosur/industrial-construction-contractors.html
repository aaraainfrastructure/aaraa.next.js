Tailored for Hosur’s Manufacturing & Automotive Supply Chain

Hosur’s position as a premier automotive, electronics, and heavy engineering cluster demands factory buildings engineered for high operational uptime and structural longevity:

  • Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) Excellence: Custom primary and secondary steel framing designed for high clear spans (up to 60m column-free), crane gantry loads (up to 30T), and rapid site erection.
  • Heavy Machine & Press Foundations: Reinforced concrete isolated block foundations with vibration dampening joints for heavy stamping presses, CNC machinery, and industrial compressors.
  • High-Flatness VNA Flooring: Laser-screed FM2 / TR34 compliant industrial concrete floors with abrasion-resistant metallic hardeners for automated warehouse material handling.
