How to Choose a Commercial Contractor in NCR

Choosing the right Commercial Contractor in NCR is vital to ensuring project longevity, structural integrity, and commercial viability across the building's lifecycle. Key selection criteria for developers include:

  • Verify In-House Engineering vs. Subcontracted Civil Execution: Confirm that the contractor employs dedicated structural and MEP engineers rather than delegating core works to unvetted subcontractors.
  • Inspect Completed Commercial References: Visit active or completed commercial building sites to evaluate concrete finish quality, curtain wall alignment, and MEP neatness.
  • Assess Structural & Material Quality Standards: Ensure the contractor uses factory-tested TMT steel bars, certified ready-mix concrete (RMC), and approved MEP brands.
  • Review Safety & Compliance Track Record: Confirm zero-fatality site records, comprehensive labor insurance, and compliance with National Building Code (NBC) standards.
  • Evaluate Financial Stability & PBG Support: Verify financial capacity to secure bank guarantees and support long-lead material procurement without project stoppage.
