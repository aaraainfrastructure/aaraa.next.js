Engineered for North Karnataka’s Solar Corridor

Kudligi and the surrounding Bellary-Chitradurga solar belt feature high solar irradiation coupled with complex geological terrain requiring robust structural civil engineering:

  • Hard-Rock & Gravel Geotechnical Piling: Kudligi’s hard-strata geology requires DTH pneumatic rock-drilling and specialized concrete piers to anchoring MMS arrays against severe wind load uplift.
  • Hybrid Wind-Solar Integration Civil Scope: Managing civil execution across co-located wind turbine foundations and expansive solar PV fields requires synchronized heavy machinery logistics and access road network construction.
  • Substation & KPTCL Evacuation Facilities: Construction of 33/110kV pooling switchyards, transformer foundation pads, oil-soak pits, and control room buildings for seamless KPTCL grid synchronization.

Turnkey Scope for Independent Power Producers (IPPs)

We provide full-spectrum engineering, procurement support, and civil/electrical execution for renewable energy IPPs in Karnataka:

  • Site Preparation & Internal Logistics: Topographic contour surveys, heavy grading, perimeter fencing, and all-weather arterial access roads.
  • Solar PV & MMS Civil Scope: MMS table erection, pile pull-out testing, DC cabling trenches, and inverter station foundations.
  • Electrical BoP & Switchyard Infrastructure: Underground HT AC cabling, outdoor breaker foundations, lightning protection masts, and SCADA control rooms.
