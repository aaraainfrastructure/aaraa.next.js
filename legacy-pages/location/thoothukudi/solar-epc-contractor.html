Engineered for Coastal & Solar Corridor Challenges

Tuticorin’s strategic location along the Gulf of Mannar presents unique environmental factors that demand specialized engineering for long-term solar asset reliability:

  • Anti-Corrosive Marine Protection: Coastal atmospheric salinity rapidly degrades standard steel. We utilize hot-dip galvanized steel structures with 80+ micron zinc coating thickness and stainless steel (SS316) hardware to guarantee 25+ year structural integrity.
  • Coastal Saline Soil Pile Engineering: High water tables and corrosive soil profiles in Thoothukudi district require specialized driven cast-in-situ concrete piles and sulphate-resistant cement formulations.
  • High Wind-Load Structural Stability: MMS structures are engineered for coastal wind speeds up to 180 km/h in full compliance with IS 875 (Part 3) standards.
