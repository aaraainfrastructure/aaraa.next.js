Project Overview & Key Highlights

AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is proud to announce the successful completion and handover of the Shell Petrol Bunk development located in Wagholi, Pune City. Executed with a total project budget of ₹1.2 Crore, the development represents a high-efficiency retail fuel station built to strict international safety and operational standards.

Delivering a commercial fuel retail station in an urban growth corridor like Wagholi requires seamless coordination across civil engineering, structural steel fabrication, subterranean tank installation, and specialized explosion-proof Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing (MEP) systems. AARAA Infrastructure delivered the entire project scope within schedule while adhering strictly to Shell's corporate brand guidelines and HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) protocols.

Comprehensive Scope of Execution

Our turnkey contract encompassed end-to-end site preparation, subterranean engineering, superstructure construction, and site finishes:

  • Substructure & Tank Pit Earthwork: Deep excavation, shoring, and RCC foundation slab construction for high-capacity underground fuel storage tanks and fuel line trenches.
  • Canopy Structure & RCC Works: Structural steel canopy fabrication, column erection, RCC pump island platforms, and integrated canopy cladding.
  • Specialized MEP Installations: Execution of explosion-proof electrical wiring, fuel pump piping conduits, site drainage networks, and automated fuel monitoring system conduits.
  • Heavy-Duty Driveway Pavement: Laying high-load interlocking paver blocks across the entire forecourt to support heavy commercial traffic and passenger vehicles.
  • Architectural & Brand Finishes: Exterior weather-resistant painting, high-visibility LED lighting masts, perimeter fencing, customer amenity building finishes, and Shell brand signage mounting.
