Strengthening Chennai's Water Future

Chennai’s growing demand for reliable water resources has made large-scale water infrastructure increasingly important. The development of advanced desalination facilities is an important part of strengthening the city’s long-term water supply infrastructure.

AARAA Infrastructure Private Limited is proud to announce its association with the 400 MLD SWRO Desalination Plant at Perur, Chennai, through a civil works package awarded by VA Tech Wabag Limited.

The project is being executed for the Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board (CMWSSB) and represents a significant infrastructure development in Tamil Nadu's coastal water management masterplan.

Scope of Civil & Allied Works

Under the ₹5.19 Crore work package awarded by prime contractor VA Tech Wabag Limited, AARAA Infrastructure is executing core building civil and structural engineering tasks, including:

  • Structural Concrete Execution: Heavy RCC raft foundations, retaining walls, pump house substructures, and water containment units designed for high-salinity coastal environments.
  • Equipment Foundations & Pedestals: Precision casting of heavy machinery pedestals for high-pressure SWRO booster pumps, filtration units, and energy recovery systems.
  • Internal Utility Corridors & Trenches: Reinforced concrete cable trenches, chemical dosing pipe ducts, and drainage channels tailored for industrial plant operations.
  • Coastal Site Engineering: Specialized concrete mix design, dewatering controls, and anti-corrosion rebar protection suitable for Perur's coastal soil chemistry.

Impact on Chennai's Water Infrastructure

Once completed, the 400 MLD SWRO plant at Perur will stand as one of the largest desalination facilities in South Asia, supplying clean drinking water to millions of residents in greater Chennai. AARAA Infrastructure’s contribution to the civil infrastructure ensures structural durability, safety compliance, and seamless integration with process units.
